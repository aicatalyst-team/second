## Line-Level Feedback
### Thesis clarity
- **Location**: Opening paragraph
- **Issue**: The first paragraph describes what Second is, but doesn't state the thesis (why this PoC matters, what's in it for the reader). The reader has to wait until paragraph 4 to understand the purpose.
- **Suggestion**: Open with a sentence that frames the challenge: deploying a multi-service AI platform on OpenShift. Then introduce Second as the test case.

### Opening hook
- **Location**: First paragraph
- **Issue**: "Second is an open source platform..." is a description, not a hook. There's no tension or promise of value.
- **Suggestion**: Lead with the problem or challenge. Something like: "Multi-service AI platforms are becoming common, but can they run on OpenShift without invasive changes? We tested that with Second."

### Section flow
- **Location**: Overall
- **Issue**: The progression is logical and clean. H2s tell a coherent story.
- **Suggestion**: No changes needed.

## Summary
The most important structural change is strengthening the opening. Move the thesis forward so readers know within 2 sentences what this post will prove and why they should care.
